How do I keep my data on a new iPhone?

BunnyBank keeps your expenses in your own iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on the new iPhone, make sure iCloud is on for BunnyBank in the Settings app (your name › iCloud), and open BunnyBank. Your data syncs back.

I bought something. How do I get it back?

In BunnyBank, go to Settings › Restore Purchases. Purchases are one-time and belong to your Apple Account, so they come back on any iPhone signed in to it.

I have a code. Where do I use it?

Go to Settings › Redeem Code and enter it. It unlocks the same things a purchase does, and restores the same way.

Receipt scanning or the Assistant says it isn't available

Reading receipts, speaking an expense and the Assistant use Apple Intelligence, which runs on your iPhone. They need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. Everything else in BunnyBank works on any supported iPhone.

How do I send a screenshot of a receipt to BunnyBank?

Take a screenshot, touch and hold the thumbnail, and choose BunnyBank in the share sheet (it may be under More the first time). Tap the notification that appears, or just open BunnyBank, and it's read like a scan.

How does it know what to suggest?

From your own records, worked out on your iPhone: where you are, the time of day and day of the week, the day of the month, today's calendar and the amounts you usually spend. Location and calendar are optional. You can reset what suggestions have learned, or erase saved locations, in Settings › Smart suggestions.

How do I use BunnyBank with Siri?

Try "Add an expense in BunnyBank", "How much have I spent this week in BunnyBank?" or, if you've made a template called Coffee, "Coffee in BunnyBank". You'll find all of BunnyBank's actions in the Shortcuts app too.

I moved to another country. How do I change currency?

Go to Settings › Home Currency and pick the new one. If you've recorded things in your old currency, BunnyBank asks what you'd like: use both currencies side by side, or switch over. Switching, you choose whether past expenses are converted at an exchange rate you type in, kept as they were, or relabelled (if they were really in the new currency all along). Converted expenses remember their original amount, so you can put them back from the same screen.

Can I see my spending in each currency?

Yes, with Multiple Currencies: the Summary gets a currency menu, and the Add form can log an expense abroad in the local currency. Without it, totals are in your home currency and anything in another currency is shown next to them.

How do I delete my data?

Delete expenses, shops and categories in the app, or erase saved locations in Settings › Smart suggestions. To remove everything, delete the app, then remove BunnyBank's data from iCloud in the Settings app (your name › iCloud › Storage). See the Privacy Policy for details.
